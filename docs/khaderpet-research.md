# Khaderpet research: how the owner's relative's wholesale shop works (2026-11-17)

The primary source is the owner's brother-in-law, who runs a women's and kids' wear wholesale shop in Khaderpet,
Tirupur. The web sources are weaker (directories and vendor pages).

## Facts from the shop

- **Orders:** 25-30 orders every two weeks (corrected by the owner on 2026-11-20; earlier notes said "a day") arrive by phone call or WhatsApp voice note. Buyers first ask for the
  catalogue, then order by phone or voice note: items, quantity, size, colour and transport.
- **Languages:** Tamil first, Malayalam second (Kerala buyers), some Hindi. The owner later added Kannada in the
  Activity 2 prompt.
- **Products:** crop tops; kids' co-ord sets; women's co-ord sets; kids' and women's shorts; palazzo sets for kids;
  full and 3/4 palazzo pants for women; leggings for kids and teen girls; pyjama pants; 3/4 pants. Also seen in the
  notebook: boys' and girls' T-shirts, shorts sets, pant sets, stone pants, kids' pants, popcorn pant sets, girls'
  pants and kids' palazzo sets.
- **Minimum quantities:**
  - kids' wear: 10 each per size;
  - crop tops: minimum 6;
  - pants and palazzos: 25 pieces as told, but the notebook shows 24 (2 dozen); the prompt used 24;
  - shorts: minimum 10.
- **Colours:** not negotiable (assorted).
- **Paperwork:** he notes orders by hand in a notebook, then converts them to a normal bill or a GST bill. Money to
  give and to collect goes into a khata (credit) book.

- **The bracketed second rates are unexplained.** Perhaps a GST rate, a newer price, or another buyer's rate. Ask him.
- **Missing from the page:** buyer name and date (blank), the size split per size, transport, and payment status.
  These live in the call, the voice note or his memory.
- **A crop-top rate of ₹75** was used in the prompt as an estimate; it is not from the notebook.

## The problem, in one sentence

One order spoken on a call becomes three handwritten records (notebook, bill, khata), re-typed by hand, with key
details (who, sizes, transport, credit) living only in a voice note or memory.

## Web context (weaker evidence)

- **Selling in lots:** Khaderpet shops usually sell in sets or lots, not single pieces
  (https://www.textileexport.in/company/textile/khaderpet-garment-market/).
- **The WhatsApp trade loop:** catalogue broadcast → voice-note order → reply → payment nudge
  (https://greentick.ai/blogs/whatsapp-api-for-wholesale-business/).
- **No independent study** says which step fails most. The relative's answers matter most.
