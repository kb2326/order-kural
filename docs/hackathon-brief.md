# Hackathon brief: India Runs on Voice (read 2026-11-17)

Sources: https://hackos.scaler.com/wispr/ (landing page) and https://hackos.scaler.com/wispr/dashboard.html
("Build for Bharat"). The dashboard contains prototype text ("Quality results and scores are simulated"), so treat
its statuses as a preview and confirm through official channels.

## Basics

- **Organisers:** Scaler and Wispr Flow.
- **Who can enter:** students and working professionals in India, aged 18 or over. Free. No prior AI or voice
  experience needed.
- **Teams:** solo or up to 3 people.
- **Theme:** voice-first tools for India, built by voice. Any Indian language.
- **Tracks:** civic services, healthcare, education, small business, everyday access.
- **City briefs:** sample briefs for Bengaluru, Madurai, Patna, Indore and Kolkata. The page does not say whether
  they feed Round 2.
- **Tools:** any editor or AI tool (the FAQ names ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor). Wispr Flow is the expected voice input.

## Prizes (the dashboard says "Prize details remain unconfirmed")

- **Cash:** ₹30 lakh across 15 teams. 1st ₹7 L, 2nd ₹5 L, 3rd ₹3 L, 4th-15th ₹1.25 L per team.
- **Top 15 teams:** 12 months of Flow Pro per member, a sponsored trip to Singapore, and a Wispr interview.
- **Round 1 builders:** 1 month of Flow Pro. Eligibility needs Wispr Flow signed up with the registration email.

## Rules and judging

- **Round 1:** an AI quality check per activity, scored out of 100. Activity 2 is worth up to 50 points toward the
  Round 1 total.
- **Round 1 score (FAQ, read 2026-11-20):** Activity 1 has no points; it is complete once the idea passes the quality
  check. The Round 1 score comes from Activities 2 (up to 50) and 3 (up to 25) and counts for 10% of the final score.
- **Later rounds:** the criteria and rules are shared when each round goes live (FAQ 05 and 07).
- **Team lock:** the FAQ says the team locks at the first submission (including the Round 1 idea). The dashboard says
  changes are allowed until Round 2 starts on 27 Nov. Follow the stricter rule.
- **Contact:** wispr.support@scaler.com and the event Discord.
